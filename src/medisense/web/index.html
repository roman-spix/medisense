Getting good readings

  • Put the board 1–2 m from where you sit, about chest height, with your body between the board and the WiFi router if you can.
  • The router should be in the same room or the next one. Breathing shows up best when the signal passes through or bounces off your chest.
  • Sit still for the first 30 seconds: the breathing number appears after about 20 seconds of quiet.
  • Keep other people and pets out of the room, and don't let fans or curtains move near the board.
  • For posture, put the laptop so the camera sees your head and shoulders (hips too if possible).
  • Heart rate from WiFi is rough. Check it against your Polar watch with “Watch heart rate” during sessions; that also helps improve MediSense later.

      About the Vairocana posture

      1. Legs crossed in vajra or half-lotus (or simply crossed), knees low.
      2. Hands in the mudra of meditative equipoise: right hand resting on the left, palms up, thumbs lightly touching, a few finger-widths below the navel.
      3. Spine straight like an arrow, or a stack of coins: upright, neither rigid nor slumped.
      4. Shoulders level and open, “like a vulture's wings”: relaxed, not hunched.
      5. Neck slightly bent, chin a little tucked, the head neither dropping nor raised.
      6. Eyes resting, gaze directed down past the tip of the nose.
      7. Mouth relaxed, lips lightly closed, the tongue resting against the upper palate.

      A dropping head often comes with dullness; raised shoulders and a pushed-forward head with tension or agitation. The reminder names what drifted so you can correct it gently.

        A healthy desk posture

        1. Screen about an arm's length away, its top at or a little below eye level. With a laptop, a stand (or a pile of books) plus a separate keyboard makes the biggest difference.
        2. Back upright and supported: sit back in the chair, hips a little higher than the knees, feet flat on the floor.
        3. Shoulders relaxed and down, elbows close to the body at about a right angle, wrists straight.
        4. Head balanced over the shoulders (ears above them), chin neutral, not pushed towards the screen.
        5. Move: stand up, stretch or walk a little every 30 to 60 minutes. No posture stays good for hours.

        The checks compare with your own good posture: calibrate once at your desk, and again after you move the laptop or the chair. A reminder comes only when a drift lasts (20 s by default), at most every few minutes.
